Problem · Assurance gap
02

The problem is not only what AI says. It is what organizations cannot prove.

Problem

AI governance often remains a collection of policies, dashboards and periodic reviews—not an enforceable assurance workflow.

Were ethical and societal risks assessed?

Were factual claims grounded in authoritative evidence?

Is Egyptian Arabic and code-switch performance reliable?

Did a qualified human review the case?

Was evidence modified after the decision?

Should this result actually be considered final?

Governance challenge: policy intent → executable controlNo achieved-result claim

06

End-to-end operational workflow.

Operational overview

Control sequence

  1. 1Validate and deduplicate; determine required assessment branches.
  2. 2Execute EIA, TEVV and DRS; escalate when HITL is required.
  3. 3Create a versioned snapshot and record every required evidence event.
  4. 4Generate proof, send the C-03 package and wait for P3.
  5. 5Release only after acknowledgement and successful proof verification.

Literature · Research gap
17

Literature review and research gap.

Research basis

Accountability & assurance

Raji et al.; TrustLLM. Lifecycle documentation plus measurable trustworthiness.

RAG evaluation

RAGAs; ARES. Context relevance, faithfulness, answer relevance and human calibration.

Hallucination & citation

Ji et al.; FActScore; ALCE; SelfCheckGPT; semantic entropy. Atomic facts, support and uncertainty.

Arabic retrieval & dialects

MIRACL; AraDiCE. General Arabic performance does not guarantee dialect reliability.

Bias evaluation

BBQ; ArGAN. Controlled bias testing requires cultural and language context.

Human-score calibration

SummEval. Compare automated metrics with expert and crowd judgements.

Tamper-evident evidence

Crosby & Wallach; RFC 9162. Hash-linked histories, Merkle inclusion and consistency proofs support independent integrity checks.

Proposed research gap

Can these components become one measurable assurance workflow?

Existing work addresses important pieces separately. P2 investigates their integration for Egypt-focused impact assessment, Arabic claim verification, dialect robustness, controlled human release and cryptographically verifiable evidence.

Research question: can an AI result be prevented from becoming final until required assessment, human review, evidence recording, proof generation and independent acknowledgement are complete?
