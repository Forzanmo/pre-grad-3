05ARCH

A clean pipeline: Profile → Decide → Assure → Act → Prove.

Architecture
01 · Profile

Student Intelligence

Arabic/English input, CV parsing, goals, constraints, Digital Twin.

02 · Discover

Opportunity Retrieval

Find relevant programs from structured and authoritative sources.

03 · Decide

Decision Engine

Eligibility, fit, readiness, gaps, what-if, and path planning.

04 · Assure

P2 Assurance Gate

Non-bypassable EIA + TEVV: grounding, Arabic robustness, bias delta.

05 · Act

Release / HITL

Pass safe decisions; route uncertain or harmful cases to human review.

06 · Prove

Evidence + Passport

Hash chain, Merkle proof, selective disclosure, C-03 evidence package.

Shared Knowledge Layer
PostgreSQL for structured requirements + OpenSearch for hybrid BM25/dense evidence retrieval.
Architecture rule
No direct Decision Engine → Student path. Every consequential recommendation crosses P2 first.

06COMPARE

MASAR is not a scholarship chatbot.

Comparison
DimensionMASARGeneral-purpose LLM / chatbot
PurposeBuilt for cross-border education mobility: discover, evaluate, plan, and progress toward real opportunities.Built to answer broad questions across many domains.
Student contextMaintains a normalized Opportunity Digital Twin: profile, goals, documents, constraints, and progress.Usually reasons mainly from the current prompt/conversation rather than a formal mobility-state model.
DecisioningUses structured requirements, deterministic eligibility rules, fit/readiness scoring, gap analysis, and what-if simulation.Primarily generates natural-language recommendations; hard rules are not inherently enforced.
TrustEvery consequential recommendation crosses a non-bypassable EIA+TEVV assurance gate for grounding, Arabic robustness, and fairness.No domain-specific assurance pipeline is inherently executed for every answer.
AccountabilityProduces auditable evidence, HITL outcomes, Merkle proofs, and selective-disclosure Opportunity Passports.A normal answer is not inherently an independently verifiable decision record.

13WHY NOW

Access to information is not enough.

Research question

Students cross countries, languages, institutions—and increasingly rely on AI to navigate them.

Cross-Border Education & Scholarships is where assurance becomes personal, immediate, and consequential.

How can we turn an AI answer from something we are asked to trust into something we can independently verify?

This question begins with scholarships—but applies anywhere an AI answer can change a real decision.

P2 Assurance & Evidence Chain

We don't ask you to trust the AI. We give you the evidence to verify it.
